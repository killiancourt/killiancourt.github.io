I am a high school student at Head-Royce School in Oakland, California, with a passion for cybersecurity, computer science, and emerging technologies.

What fascinates me most about cybersecurity is the opportunity to understand how systems work by exploring how they can fail. From investigating vulnerabilities and analyzing encryption to exploring the security implications of artificial intelligence, I enjoy looking beyond the intended functionality of technology and questioning the assumptions behind its design.

I am the founder and president of the Head-Royce Cybersecurity Club, where I promote cybersecurity education through hands-on learning and collaborative exploration. I am also a member of Next Gen Cyber Club, through which I participate in national cybersecurity competitions, community education initiatives, and conversations with cybersecurity professionals.

Beyond the technical aspects of cybersecurity, I am interested in its intersection with national security, public policy, economics, and society. Through my blog and Fireside Chats, I hope to document my learning, investigate emerging security challenges, and explore different perspectives through conversations with researchers, industry practitioners, and policymakers.

Outside of technology, I enjoy playing competitive soccer, which has taught me the importance of discipline, adaptability, and teamwork.

This website serves as a record of my intellectual journey—a place to share what I learn, document what I build, and explore questions that I have yet to answer.
